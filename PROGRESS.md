17. Real blocker: Camoufox refuses to execute inline <script> elements at all. Proven on about:blank and on a data: URL (no site policy involved): a freshly injected inline script leaves its flag at 0 while (0,eval) works. Because Next.js App Router bootstraps through 10-11 inline scripts ((self.__next_f=self.__next_f||[]).push(...)), window.__next_f stays undefined, React never mounts (0 __react* keys), window.turnstile stays undefined, the Turnstile widget never mounts, and the submit button keeps its server-rendered disabled state. The document CSP seen on the 403 hop is the challenge page's, not the app's.
